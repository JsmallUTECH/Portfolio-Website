The Richest Man in Babylon

The chapters are from the book "TThe Richest Man in Babylon", by author George S. Clason in 1926.

Seven Cures for a Lean Purse

For this chapter, the message was about seven principles of achieveing financial stability. George S. Clason, the author of this book, explains that to achieve this persons need to start saving at least 10% of their income when they get paid. This taught me to make it a habit to putting away some of my money that get for school. The second cure was to "Control thy expentdiures" simply meaning to learn how to separate our wants for our needs. Sometimes I spend alot of money on things that I want and not prioritzing things that I need and this taught me to stop doing that. The chapter then highlights that we should not only just save, but to also invest our money to build our wealth. The taught me that I should start learning more about investments.

The fourth cure speaks protecting ourselves from making bad investments. I learnt that I should carefully assess the types of investments that I make instead of jumping into an invesment because of quick profit. The fifth principle talks about the financial benfits of owning a home. This chapter taught me that home ownership is a part of long-term financial planning. The next cure speaks preparing financially when we get old and for helping other family members. Once I start working, I'll ensure that I develop a saving plan to secure me for the future. The final cure is "Increase thy ability to earn" which encourages persons to continue building on their skill. Despite my goal of wanting to become a Software Engineer, I want to enchance my programming skills so that I'm able to choose from any career paths.

Five Laws of Gold

The second chapter, "The Five Laws of Gold," speaks about five principles that help us save, invest and protect our money. The first law encourages us to save at least ten percent of our earnings. Just like the chapter previous, this taught me to make saving a habit, even when I receive little money. The second law speaks about investing our money to earn more. This taught me that I should learn more about investments to build my wealth in the future.

The third law highlights the importance of seeking advice from people who have experience in managing money. I learnt that I should seek advice before making important financial decisions. The fourth law warns us against investing in things we do not understand. This taught me to always do my research before making an investment. The final law warns us about opportunities promising quick and unrealistic profits. This taught me to be careful about the financial opportunities I see, especially on social media, instead of risking my money for quick profits.

My Biggest Takeaway

My biggest takeaway from both chapters is the importance of investing our money wisely. Before reading these chapters, I mainly thought about saving money rather than finding ways to make my money grow. Both chapters taught me that saving is important, but investing is also a way to build wealth and become financially stable. I also learnt that investing comes with risks, so I should always do my research and seek advice from people who have experience in investments. Although I'm still a university student, I want to start learning more about investments so that when I start earning my own money, I'll be able to make better financial decisions and secure my future.
