My Personality Traits and Hobbies

1. Being Organised

I take being organised seriously because I believe that keeping my work neat and structured makes everything easier to find and understand. As a university student, being organised helps me manage my assignments, notes, and study materials.

2. Watching Movies

One of my favourite hobbies is watching movies. University life can sometimes (ALL THE TIME) be stressful, especially when I have several assignments to complete. Watching movies helps me relax and take a break from my academic responsibilities.

3. Eagerness to Learn

I am always eager to learn something new. I enjoy gaining knowledge and developing new skills. This trait is important to me whether is is something related to my studies or a personal interest.

My Thoughts on AI and Learning

As a Computer Science student, I believe that AI should be used to aid learning rather take over how we think independently. AI tool chatgpt, claude and copilot are really helpful when students need help understanding difficult topics. However, I believe that total dependence on AI tools can hinder a student's ability to think critically and solve problems independently.

When I used AI tools to help me with my assigrments I usually ask it to explain it in depth so that I have a clear understanding of the topic. I also use AI tools to help me with coding of what is being asked in my assignments. I also use it when I'm studying to geneterate practice questions and then answer it myself to help me prepare for my exams. UNESCO (2023), highlights the importance of using generative AI responsibly in education while protecting human agency and encouraging meaningful learning.

Despite the benefits of AI tools, I believe that studentshould also take into account that AI tools aren't always accurate and can sometimes provide incorrect information. Kasneci et al. (2023) discuss both the educational opportunities and challenges associated with large language models. So, when students choose to use this tools, they should ensure that the information it is providing is accurate and reliable.
